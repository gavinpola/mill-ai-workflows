# MECE Opportunity Scoring Framework

## Purpose
A systematic, reusable framework for scoring and prioritizing enterprise targets for Mill's commercial expansion. This ensures consistent evaluation and data-driven decision making.

---

## Scoring Dimensions

### 1. Market Size (25% weight)
**Question:** How big is the opportunity?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | >$50M annual food procurement, >500 locations |
| 4 | $20-50M procurement, 200-500 locations |
| 3 | $5-20M procurement, 50-200 locations |
| 2 | $1-5M procurement, 10-50 locations |
| 1 | <$1M procurement, <10 locations |

**Data sources:**
- Annual reports, 10-K filings
- Industry databases
- Press releases on expansion
- Estimated from location count × average spend

---

### 2. Sustainability Commitment (20% weight)
**Question:** How serious are they about sustainability?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Public net-zero commitment, dedicated sustainability team, active food waste programs |
| 4 | ESG report published, sustainability goals stated, some programs in place |
| 3 | Sustainability mentioned in messaging, no concrete programs |
| 2 | Minimal sustainability focus, reactive only |
| 1 | No apparent sustainability initiative |

**Data sources:**
- ESG/sustainability reports
- Press releases
- Executive interviews
- CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project) filings

---

### 3. Operational Fit (20% weight)
**Question:** How well does Mill fit their operations?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Significant on-site food prep, high waste visibility, tech-forward operations |
| 4 | Moderate food prep, some waste tracking, open to new tech |
| 3 | Limited food prep, minimal waste visibility |
| 2 | Pre-packaged focus, little on-site waste generation |
| 1 | No relevant food operations |

**Data sources:**
- Store/facility formats
- Operational descriptions
- Competitor implementations
- Industry analysis

---

### 4. Strategic Value (20% weight)
**Question:** What's the strategic upside beyond revenue?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Category leader, would unlock entire industry, strong PR value |
| 4 | Major player, good reference potential, industry influence |
| 3 | Solid mid-market player, moderate reference value |
| 2 | Niche player, limited reference value |
| 1 | No strategic value beyond direct revenue |

**Data sources:**
- Market position analysis
- Brand recognition
- Industry influence assessment
- PR/media presence

---

### 5. Accessibility (15% weight)
**Question:** How easy is it to reach and close?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 5 | Existing relationship, known decision makers, short sales cycle |
| 4 | Warm connection possible, clear decision maker, reasonable cycle |
| 3 | Cold outreach needed, decision maker identifiable |
| 2 | Complex buying process, multiple stakeholders |
| 1 | No clear path to decision maker, very long cycle |

**Data sources:**
- LinkedIn connections
- Conference attendance
- Board/investor overlap
- Sales cycle research

---

## Scoring Formula

```
Total Score = (Market × 0.25) + (Sustainability × 0.20) + (Operations × 0.20) + (Strategic × 0.20) + (Access × 0.15)

Max Score: 5.00
```

---

## Score Interpretation

| Score Range | Priority | Action |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| 4.0 - 5.0 | **Tier 1** | Immediate pursuit, full resources |
| 3.0 - 3.9 | **Tier 2** | Active pursuit, standard resources |
| 2.0 - 2.9 | **Tier 3** | Opportunistic, low resource allocation |
| 1.0 - 1.9 | **Deprioritize** | Not a fit at this time |
