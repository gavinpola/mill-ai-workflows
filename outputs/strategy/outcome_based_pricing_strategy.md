## 1. The Attribution Challenge

Harry correctly identified the core challenge: **How do we attribute procurement savings to Mill's data?**

### The Problem
Procurement savings are notoriously difficult to measure. Companies lose 30-60% of negotiated savings between contract signature and invoice payment due to:
- Baseline disputes
- Volume shifts
- Price inflation
- Confounding variables (menu changes, staffing, seasonality)

### Baseline Methodology Options

| Method | Formula | Best For | Mill Application |
|--------|---------|----------|------------------|
| **Budget-based** | Budget - Actual = Savings | Annual planning cycles | Limited—too static |
| **Market benchmark** | Market Price - Achieved × Volume | When indices exist | **Recommended** |
| **Historical** | Past Baseline - Current = Savings | YoY comparisons | Good for pilots |
| **Technical** | Engineering change = Cost reduction | Spec changes | Process improvements |

### Recommendation: Market Benchmark Approach

For Mill Commercial, use **market benchmark baselines**:

1. **Pre-deployment:** Capture 60-90 days of waste data (type, volume, timing) before any intervention
2. **Establish baseline:** Calculate food cost per waste category against industry benchmarks for comparable operations
3. **Post-deployment:** Measure same metrics with Mill insights active
4. **Attribution:** Savings = (Baseline waste cost) - (Post-Mill waste cost), adjusted for confounders

**Critical:** Document baseline methodology BEFORE deployment. This single step eliminates 60%+ of post-hoc disputes.

## 2. Pricing Model Recommendation: Hybrid

Pure outcome-based pricing is risky for both vendor and customer. The industry consensus (73% of outcome-based SaaS) uses **hybrid models**.

### Proposed Structure

| Component | Description | Purpose |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Base Subscription** | Software + implementation + support + hardware | Predictable revenue; covers costs |
| **Outcome Bonus** | % of verified procurement savings above baseline | Aligns incentives; captures upside |

### Why Hybrid?

**For Mill:**
- Base fee ensures unit economics work even if savings take time to materialize
- Outcome bonus creates urgency and partnership mindset
- Reduces sales cycle risk (customers not bearing all uncertainty)

**For Customers:**
- Predictable baseline cost for budgeting
- Upside sharing feels fair—"we profit together"
- Lower perceived risk than pure subscription (if savings don't materialize, they're not overpaying)

### Competitive Context

| Company | Model | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| **Winnow** | Subscription with outcome guarantees | Targets businesses with £1M+ food budget, 10+ sites |
| **Leanpath** | Subscription with validated ROI reporting | 2-7x ROI, 50% waste reduction typical |
| **Orbisk** | Subscription (plug-and-play) | 70% waste reduction claim |

Mill can differentiate by being more explicit about shared risk.

## 4. Go-to-Market Without Overpromising

The biggest risk in outcome-based pricing is overpromising. Here's how to avoid it.

### What We Control vs. What We Don't

| Mill Controls | Customer Controls |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Waste detection accuracy | Staff engagement with insights |
| Data quality and timeliness | Menu consistency |
| Insight generation | Procurement decision-making |
| System uptime | Operational discipline |

### Positioning Strategy

**Lead with conservative estimates:**
- Say "2-4% food cost savings" not "up to 10%"
- Use lower-bound case studies
- Cite third-party validation where possible

**Be transparent about variables:**
- "These results depend on: baseline accuracy, staff engagement, menu consistency"
- "Mill delivers the insights—you decide how to act on them"

**Tiered guarantees based on commitment:**
- Basic tier: Data only, no guarantees
- Standard tier: Data + recommendations, soft savings targets
- Premium tier: Full partnership, shared risk/reward, guaranteed savings floor

### What NOT to Promise

- Specific dollar savings without baseline
- Timelines for ROI without pilot data
- Results dependent on customer execution
- Attribution for factors outside Mill's control
