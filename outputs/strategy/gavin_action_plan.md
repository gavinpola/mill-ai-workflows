## Executive Summary

Mill is transforming from a hardware company into an autonomous data intelligence platform. This roadmap outlines the strategic path from our current state to a $10B food intelligence platform that makes the entire food system smarter.

**The Core Thesis:** Turn food waste data into autonomous procurement decisions.
