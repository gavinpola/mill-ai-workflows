The data tells you exactly what to change

Example insights from the Mill dashboard

Rotisserie timing

Last 3 rotisserie batches are consistently wasted. Recommendation: Move final cook time 45 minutes earlier.

Pizza dough prep

Tuesday prep waste 2.3x other days. Likely overstocking for expected demand that doesn't materialize.

Produce spoilage

Lettuce waste spikes 3 days after delivery. Consider more frequent, smaller orders.

End-of-day patterns

Hot food waste highest 8-9 PM. Reduce evening holding inventory by 20% without affecting availability.

Seasonal variance

Summer pizza demand 15% lower than winter. Adjust dough prep seasonally to reduce waste.

Training opportunity

Location 127 waste is 2x peer average. Likely staff training issue—schedule intervention.

Costco's circular opportunity: food scraps become chicken feed

Mill's unique value beyond waste reduction

Mill doesn't just track waste—it processes it. Food scraps are dehydrated and converted into nutrient-rich feed ingredient.

For Costco, this creates a circular loop: food waste from food courts can feed the chickens that become Costco rotisserie chickens.

Why this matters

Costco sells 100+ million rotisserie chickens annually. Closing the loop on food waste aligns with member expectations and ESG commitments.
