# Costco + Mill: Speaker Notes

## Deck Overview
- **Total slides:** 16
- **Recommended time:** 20-25 minutes + 10 min Q&A
- **Key message:** Waste data is procurement intelligence

---

## Slide-by-Slide Notes

### Slide 1: Cover
- Thank them for their time
- Mention we're excited about Costco's zero waste 2030 commitment
- Frame this as a partnership exploration, not a sales pitch

### Slide 2: The Problem - Scale
- **Key stat:** 40% of all food in America is wasted
- Food waste generates more emissions than aviation
- Pause on "Food waste is #1 material in landfills" - let it land

### Slide 3: Costco Specific
- Reference their scale: 890+ warehouses, $254B revenue
- The rotisserie chicken stat (117M/year) humanizes the scale
- **Transition:** "High food prep = high waste visibility opportunity" - frame this as a strength, not a problem

### Slide 4: The Hidden Cost
- This is the "aha" slide
- Most companies measure diversion (downstream)
- Few measure what/when/why (upstream)
- **Key line:** "Without granular waste data, you're optimizing blind"

### Slide 5: The Opportunity
- Reframe: waste stream → procurement intelligence system
- The $50-100M number will get attention - be ready to defend it
- Connect to their 2030 deadline

### Slide 6: Mill Solution Overview
- Keep it simple: Capture → Analyze → Optimize → Close loop
- Emphasize data outputs over hardware
- The "chicken feed" angle is the sustainability story

### Slide 7: The Data Advantage
- Use the quote: "If you can't measure it, you can't improve it"
- The table shows the difference between vague and actionable
- **Example:** "Store #427 wastes 40% less than #312" - this specificity is what they don't have today

### Slide 8: Supply Chain Optimization
- **THIS IS THE KEY SLIDE**
- Walk through the timeline slowly
- Emphasize: "Not just better disposal - less waste generated at source"
- This is why Mill is a data company, not a waste company

### Slide 9: Proof Point - Amazon/Whole Foods
- Peer validation is powerful
- **Don't oversell:** "They're seeing early results" not "They've transformed everything"
- Competitive angle: "Don't fall behind"

### Slide 10: Google Partnership
- Shows breadth beyond retail
- The "Costco?" box creates aspiration
- **Key line:** "The question is whether Costco leads or follows"

### Slide 11: Custom ROI Model
- Walk through assumptions slowly
- **15-25% reduction** is conservative - say so
- **Procurement optimization uplift** is the multiplier
- Offer to do custom modeling with their data

### Slide 12: Implementation Roadmap
- Start small, prove value, scale fast
- **"First insights within 30 days"** - set expectations
- This de-risks their decision

### Slide 13: Partnership Models
- Always recommend Option A (pilot) first
- Let results sell the bigger deal
- Don't push for strategic partnership in first meeting

### Slide 14: Why Now
- Create urgency without pressure
- All factual statements leading to obvious conclusion
- The 2030 deadline is real

### Slide 15: Call to Action
- Ask for the specific next step: technical deep-dive
- List what you'll cover - reduces uncertainty
- Don't oversell, just get the meeting

### Slide 16: Contact
- End with mission, not transaction
- Leave them thinking about bigger picture

## Objection Handling

### "We already have waste programs"
> "That's great - you're ahead of most. Mill isn't about replacing your programs, it's about making them smarter. The question is: do you know specifically what's being wasted, when, and why? That's the intelligence gap Mill fills."

### "This seems expensive"
> "Let me flip that. What's the cost of continuing to optimize blind? We're talking about turning a cost center into a data asset. The ROI isn't just in waste reduction - it's in the procurement intelligence."

### "Amazon is a competitor - why would we work with the same vendor?"
> "Fair question. Two things: First, the data is yours - we don't share insights between customers. Second, if Amazon is investing in this, that validates the approach. The question is whether Costco wants to be in front of that curve or behind it."

### "We need to think about it"
> "Of course. What would be most helpful for your decision? A deeper technical dive? Custom ROI modeling with your data? We're happy to invest the time to make this decision easy."
