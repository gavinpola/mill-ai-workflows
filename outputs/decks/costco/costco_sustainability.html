True circular economy: food waste becomes chicken feed

A unique angle for Costco's vertically integrated supply chain

Mill doesn't just track waste, it processes it. Our technology dehydrates food scraps into nutrient-rich material suitable for animal feed.

For Costco, this creates a potential closed loop: food waste from food courts could feed the chickens that become rotisserie chickens.

Why this matters for ESG

Complete circularity is the gold standard. "Waste becomes input" is a more powerful story than "waste diverted from landfill."
