2. **Tech-Forward**: Already using AI for inventory management, culturally receptive to technology solutions

5. **Supply Chain Value**: High procurement volume means waste data → procurement optimization is highly valuable
6. **Accessibility**: Clearer decision-making than franchise hospitality

### Differentiated Pitch for Costco

**Don't lead with:** "Reduce food waste, save money"
**Do lead with:** "Use waste data to optimize your supply chain and procurement"

Key Messages:
1. Mill gives you intelligence on what's being wasted across all warehouses in real-time

3. Whole Foods is already doing this with Amazon - don't fall behind
4. Your zero waste 2030 goal needs a data platform - Mill is it
