How should we see food waste?

12 engineering approaches for classifying food waste in commercial kitchens. Ranked by accuracy, friction, feasibility, cost, maintenance, and differentiation.

01 — The challenge

Food waste classification is hard

When a kitchen worker dumps a plate of scraps, we need to identify what's being thrown away. This data becomes procurement intelligence. But the physics are challenging.

🍝

Mixed pile occlusion

Food gets dumped as a pile. Items on top hide items below. A single camera angle misses 30-50% of the waste.

⚡

Zero friction tolerance

Back-of-house staff can't slow down. Any system that changes their workflow will fail adoption. 10/10 friction score or nothing.

💡

Variable conditions

Kitchen lighting changes. Surfaces are wet, greasy, reflective. Items are crushed, mixed, steaming. No controlled environment.

🎯

85%+ accuracy required

Below 85%, the data isn't actionable. Procurement decisions need confidence. False positives erode trust.
