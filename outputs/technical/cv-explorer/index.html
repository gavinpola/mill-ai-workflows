Compare all 12 designs vs baseline

Every alternative is evaluated against the baseline system. Cost shown as delta from ~$2,947 baseline.

Design What it adds Cost Δ Best for
BASELINE Sculptor RGB + depth + structured light (current design) — What we're shipping. 85%+ accuracy, volume estimation.
Kaleidoscope Mirror array for 4-6 simultaneous views Same Multi-angle capture from single camera. Patentable.
Weighmaster High-frequency load cells (100+ Hz) +5% Validation layer. Weight signatures cross-check vision.
Listener MEMS microphones for acoustic sensing +2% Very cheap add-on. Novel physics channel. $5/mic.
Photo Booth Vertical tunnel with 360° cameras +30% Highest accuracy (92-97%). Gravity separates items.
Tumbler Rotating sieve for mechanical separation +40% Your original idea. Creative, but jam risk.
Cascade Inclined conveyor for temporal spread +50% Converts instant dump to time-spread capture.
Heat Map LWIR thermal imaging +20% Fresh vs cold waste. Works in any lighting.
Fingerprinter NIR spectral imaging +25% Material chemistry (water, sugar, protein).
Scanner Capacitive/RF sensing in bin walls +15% See through occlusion. No line-of-sight needed.
Polarizer Polarimetric imaging +80% Detect plastic contamination. Cut glare.
Penetrator mmWave radar +20% Penetrate surface. See through pile.
